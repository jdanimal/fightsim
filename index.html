Fightsim

What is Fightsim?

Fightsim makes short fight-simulation videos: original characters with picture-themed powers bounce around an arena until one is left standing. The videos are generated by a personal program and published as YouTube Shorts.

The Fightsim uploader

The "Fightsim" Google app is a small desktop tool used only by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload the generated videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, with their title and description. It is not offered to other users.
